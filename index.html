Shipping
& Returns.

Where your order is, and what happens if something's wrong with it.

🚚
Delivery Timeline

Orders are processed once payment has been successfully confirmed. Most orders leave us within 1–2 business days of that confirmation.

Delivery across Nigeria typically takes 3–7 business days, depending on your state — the exact shipping fee and estimated delivery destination is shown at checkout when you select your state.

Delivery times may run longer for remote locations or during periods of high order volume (like drop days). Please make sure your delivery address and phone number are correct at checkout — we ship to the details you provide.

↩
Refund Policy

Every piece is inspected before it's dispatched to make sure it meets our quality standard. If your item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, we'll make it right.

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order reference and clear photos of the item, and we'll take it from there.

Approved refunds are processed back through your original payment method. Timing after that depends on your bank or payment provider, and can take a few business days to reflect.
